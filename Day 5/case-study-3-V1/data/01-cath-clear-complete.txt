Cardiac catheterization performed via right femoral access without complication. Left ventriculography demonstrated an ejection fraction estimated at 55%, within normal limits. Coronary angiography revealed severe stenosis of the proximal left anterior descending artery, estimated at 90% narrowing. Right coronary artery and circumflex were free of significant disease. Troponin I level: 0.03 ng/mL, within normal reference range. Patient tolerated the procedure well and was transferred to recovery in stable condition.
