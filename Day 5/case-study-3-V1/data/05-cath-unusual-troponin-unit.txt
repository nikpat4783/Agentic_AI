Cardiac catheterization performed without complication. Ventriculography demonstrated an ejection fraction of 58%, normal systolic function. Coronary angiography showed severe stenosis of the right coronary artery, estimated 88%. High-sensitivity troponin T was reported by the outside laboratory as 45 pg/mL, which the reporting lab flagged as mildly elevated for their assay's reference range. Patient tolerated the procedure well and was discharged to the observation unit for monitoring.
