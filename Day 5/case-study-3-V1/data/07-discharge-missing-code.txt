Patient was admitted for evaluation of shortness of breath and lower extremity swelling. Clinical picture and echocardiogram findings were consistent with an exacerbation of congestive heart failure. Diuresis was initiated and the patient responded well over the course of admission, with resolution of swelling and improved breathing. The coding team has not yet finalized the discharge diagnosis classification for this encounter; that will be completed in a separate addendum.
