Patient admitted with diabetic ketoacidosis. Primary diagnosis: type 1 diabetes mellitus with ketoacidosis, ICD-10 code E10.10. Secondary diagnosis of hypertension, ICD-10 code I10, was also managed during this admission with no changes to home regimen. Patient's blood glucose and acid-base status normalized with insulin drip and fluid resuscitation, and she was transitioned back to her home insulin regimen without complication. Discharged home in stable condition with endocrinology follow-up in two weeks.
