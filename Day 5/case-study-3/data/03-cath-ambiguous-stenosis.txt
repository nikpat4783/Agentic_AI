Cardiac catheterization performed for evaluation of chest pain. Left ventriculography demonstrated an ejection fraction of 50%, mildly reduced. Coronary angiography of the left circumflex artery showed a borderline lesion that could not be definitively classified on this view -- appearance is consistent with anywhere from moderate to severe stenosis, and further evaluation with intravascular ultrasound is recommended before any intervention decision is made. Troponin 0.02 ng/mL. No acute complications during the procedure.
